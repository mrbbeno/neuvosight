Frequently asked

Straight answers on methodology, fit, and how a collaboration actually runs.

Our approach is built on Evidence-Based Validation. We combine engineering rigor (STEM background) with Lean Startup and Design Thinking, evaluating every innovation through Desirability, Feasibility, and Viability. Unlike traditional consultancies, we provide an auditable framework that delivers objective go/no-go recommendations based on real market data and technical stress-tests.
In the current "pre-seed crisis," 90–95% of ideas fail due to a lack of rigorous validation. Innovation is no longer discretionary. It's a strategic survival mechanism. With tightening capital and shifting EU/global cycles (DefTech, AI), companies and investors need a filter to kill non-viable ideas early, protecting capital and reputation while accelerating high-potential winners.
We're open to both project-based collaborations and retainer models. Every engagement solves a specific problem with a clear, high-impact output: tailored expertise rather than a one-size-fits-all framework, no initial commitment: everything starts with a 30-minute discovery conversation, no deck, no cost, and full confidentiality from the very first minute. We meet you exactly where you are and scale our involvement accordingly.
The right moment is before a larger commitment lands on the table, while the evidence is still incomplete and the decision is still genuinely open. We work most often with industrial SMEs and mid-sized manufacturers, corporate innovation units missing a reliable go/kill mechanism, university Technology Transfer Offices, and startups preparing for a funding round or corporate pilot. We're probably not the right fit for a long-term strategy retainer or a partner who avoids difficult recommendations to keep things comfortable.
The decision is always yours. The evidence is always ours. Before each phase begins, we agree on explicit criteria for what "good enough to continue" looks like, and what should trigger a pivot or stop. Working inside a large industrial organisation, every project we recommended stopping was stopped, and every project we recommended continuing reached pilot stage. A kill recommendation isn't failure. It's often the most capital-efficient result a validation process can produce.
Three areas: venture clienting & startup scouting: finding the startup that already solved your challenge and structuring the pilot, including a shared Venture Clienting Squad for SMEs; innovation validation: a structured program to find out whether an opportunity is real before resources get committed; and corporate innovation advisory: structuring decision gates, kill criteria, and organisational fit.
Three things: we deliver decisions, not frameworks: a go/no-go recommendation, a validated pilot design, a kill memo at every stage; we've actually operated from the inside, having built and run the internal startup accelerator of a large industrial company; and we understand why innovation projects actually die: organizational misfit kills more projects than bad ideas do, and spotting that mismatch early is half the work.
Deepest experience at the intersection of industrial manufacturing, electronics, and automotive supply chain, and B2B deep-tech more broadly. Geographically focused on Europe, DACH most active, alongside Spain, Italy, Poland, and the Czech Republic. We work in English and German, mostly combining remote work with on-site sessions. Sweet spot: €20M–€500M revenue range.
Before any information changes hands, we sign NDAs and confidentiality agreements. We never reuse or repurpose client-specific material across projects, and outputs are exclusively owned by the client unless otherwise agreed. Where AI tools are used, no client data goes into model training and no information leaks into shared datasets. Practices align with EU data protection requirements and the ISO 56000 series on innovation management.
