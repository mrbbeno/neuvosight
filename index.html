The neuvosight method

Gate-based, evidence-driven system. Each stage delivers a concrete output: not a report, a decision.

01

Idea Triage

Days, not weeks

Rapid feasibility scan. Technical red flags, strategic misalignment, and fundamental market signals surfaced before any significant resource commitment.

Output

Go / No-Go recommendation

02

Assumptions → evidence

Evidence-based de-risking of the core value proposition. Customer interviews, willingness-to-pay proxies, competitive positioning, moving from assumptions to auditable signals.

Output

Validation Dossier, decision-ready

Frequently asked

Straight answers on methodology, fit, and how a collaboration actually runs.

Neuvosight runs structured validation for companies deciding whether a new technology, product line or startup pilot is worth committing to. Work is organised into four gates, and each gate ends in a go, pivot or stop recommendation with the supporting evidence.

We also run venture clienting and startup scouting: when a company has a specific technology problem and suspects a startup has already solved it, we find that startup, structure the pilot and manage it through to an operational decision.

Small and mid-sized industrial companies, from roughly 15 employees upwards. Our deepest experience is in electronics manufacturing, industrial equipment and the automotive supply chain, and we work across B2B deep tech more broadly.

We are probably not the right fit if you want a long-term strategy retainer, a large implementation team, or a partner who avoids uncomfortable recommendations.

With a 30-minute conversation. No cost, no deck, no commitment. We ask what decision is in front of you and tell you honestly whether we can help.

If we can, we scope a specific piece of work with a defined output. If we can't, we say so and point you somewhere more useful.

We work project-based, priced against a defined output rather than an open-ended retainer. A triage engagement is measured in days; a full validation programme runs over weeks.

You get a fixed scope and a fixed price before the work starts.

The decision is always yours. The evidence is always ours. Before each stage begins we agree explicitly what "good enough to continue" looks like and what should trigger a pivot or a stop.

That means the recommendation at the end of each gate rests on something auditable rather than on gut feel or relationship dynamics. An external team with agreed criteria and no stake in the outcome is structurally better placed to make that call honestly than an internal team that has lived with the project for months.

Three things. We deliver decisions rather than frameworks: a go/no-go recommendation, a validated pilot design, a kill memo. We have operated from the inside, having built and run a large manufacturer's internal accelerator rather than advising on one. And we treat organisational fit as a first-order question.

The last point matters more than it sounds. The usual explanation for a failed innovation project is that the market wasn't there. In our experience, organisational misfit kills more projects than bad ideas do: a strong opportunity is invisible inside a company that expects returns early-stage reality cannot deliver, or that has no way to open a new sales channel.

A shared scouting and piloting programme for a group of non-competing companies. Each member brings its own technology challenges; the cost of scouting, screening and pilot management is spread across the group.

It also makes the programme more attractive to startups, because they are talking to several potential pilot partners at once rather than one.

Two, both free and open without an account. Valumetric is a valuation engine that produces a DCF, VC method valuation, comparables and diagnostics from a handful of inputs, in idea, fundraise and M&A modes. The Venture Readiness Quiz is a three-minute assessment scoring an idea's market readiness across four dimensions.

Both are built by our own team and reflect how we work. Neither is a lead-capture device with a tool attached.

Industrially, our deepest experience is at the intersection of manufacturing, electronics and the automotive supply chain. Geographically we work across Europe, with most activity in Central Europe and the DACH region. We work in English.

Engagements usually combine remote work with on-site sessions, so distance is rarely a blocker.

NDAs and confidentiality agreements are signed before information changes hands. Nothing client-specific is reused or shared across projects, and the outputs we produce together are exclusively yours unless you agree otherwise.

Where we use AI tools, no client data goes into model training and nothing enters shared or public datasets. Our practices align with EU data protection requirements and the ISO 56000 series on innovation management.

A triage takes days. Problem and value validation typically runs four to eight weeks depending on how many customer conversations the evidence requires. A full programme through to pilot readiness runs several months.

We agree the timeline and the stage criteria before starting, and each gate is a genuine decision point where you can stop.

Yes, and that is the common case. We are usually brought in for the parts internal teams find structurally hardest: setting decision gates, defining kill criteria, and judging whether an opportunity fits the organisation and not just the market.

We do not replace an internal team, and we do not need one to exist before we can start.
