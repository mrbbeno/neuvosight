Telemetry Insights

AI-powered performance analytics for sensor-driven decision support.

Industry

Mobility Tech / Data Analytics / AI

Early-stage startup, 8-person team

Market

Engagement type

Product Strategy & Technical Leadership

Duration

Key milestones

Launched SaaS, secured funding, built partnerships

The Challenge

Users generated large volumes of telemetry data but lacked efficient tools to interpret it and translate it into meaningful actions. The key challenge was bridging the gap between complex time-series analytics and clear, actionable recommendations that could support informed decision-making.

From a business perspective, the project required balancing rapid product development, customer validation, investor expectations, and strategic alignment while progressing through multiple innovation and accelerator programs.

The Approach

A customer-centric, data-driven methodology.

Customer discovery

Extensive user interviews to validate market needs and define the MVP around the highest-value use cases.

Iterative product development

Incremental releases supported by continuous testing and user validation to maximize reliability and usability.

Innovation & mentorship

Leveraged accelerator and innovation programs to refine product strategy, strengthen market positioning, and accelerate execution.

Feedback-driven roadmap

Product priorities determined by customer insights and usage analytics, enabling continuous refinement of AI capabilities.

The Result

18

Months from concept to a scalable AI-powered SaaS platform

4,000

Users in a growing international base

EU-wide

Strategic industry partnerships established

The project attracted external investment and demonstrated the ability to process high-volume telemetry while delivering personalized, explainable recommendations. Although the venture concluded following a strategic shift in the founders' long-term objectives, the engagement provided valuable experience in AI product development, machine learning, cloud-native architecture, and bringing a data-intensive product from concept to market.
