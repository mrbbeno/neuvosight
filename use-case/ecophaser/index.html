Phase-change climate ceilings for buildings.

Industry

Energy Efficiency / Building Technology

Company size

Three-person founding team + technical support

Market

Construction companies, public building owners

Engagement type

Co-founder / Technology & business development

Duration

4 years

Key milestone

Live field trial at Bcombinator HQ, Barcelona

The Challenge

Buildings account for a major share of global energy use, and cooling is among the largest and fastest-growing contributors. Traditional HVAC systems regulate temperature by blowing hot or cold air, which causes drafts, noise, and temperature fluctuations, effectively forcing a trade-off between efficiency and comfort.

Building owners want lower electricity bills and simpler subsystem management, while occupants expect quiet, stable, comfortable conditions, in existing buildings as well as new construction. Meeting both required a fundamentally different approach to climate control.

The Approach

Passive materials science combined with intelligent, demand-responsive control.

Phase-change materials at the core

Integrated into the suspended climate ceiling, they store heat as indoor temperatures rise and release it as they fall, stabilizing climate without drafts or noise, cutting energy for both cooling and heating.

Night charging, day cooling

Ceiling panels are cooled and "charged" overnight, then absorb excess heat during occupied hours, shifting climate load away from peak periods.

AI-driven optimization

Operation is optimized each night based on forecasted outdoor temperatures, expected occupancy, and real-time electricity prices, running only when conditions and tariffs are most favorable.

Integrated Building Management System

Centralizes climate, lighting, low-voltage, audio-video, and emergency systems into a single smart building ecosystem, tailored and expandable to client needs.

The Result

Validated in a live field trial at the Bcombinator headquarters in Barcelona, using identical upper floors as phase-change and reference levels for precise, floor-by-floor energy measurement.

42.4%

Daily energy reduction, hot-day / warm-night coastal conditions

62.7%

Cost savings, hot-day / cool-night inland conditions

35–60%

Cooling energy demand reduction, depending on configuration

30–40%

Lighting energy consumption reduction

Users reported exceptional thermal comfort and silent, draft-free operation. Acoustic performance exceeded expectations. Employees did not notice any change to the suspended ceiling, even in an office without sound-absorbing surfaces.
