Sports Technology / MedTech

Unibite

A smart mouthguard for contact sports.

Industry

Sports Technology / MedTech

Company size

3-person founding team

Market

Hungary, Austria, Denmark

Engagement type

Venture building mentorship

Duration

9 months

Key milestone

Working prototype in live training environments

The Challenge

Unibite was born from a collaboration between three university students, an engineer, a physician, and a national-level wrestler, tackling a genuine insider problem in contact sports: undetected head impacts and concussions. The smart mouthguard measures pulse, blood oxygen levels, and 6-axis impact force in real time, giving coaches and athletes actionable data during training.

As first-time founders, they lacked hands-on intuition to commercialize their vision in a highly regulated space without existing market relationships. The critical decision: choosing between professional athletes, amateur clubs, consumers, or federations.

The Approach

Months 1–3

Foundation Phase

Market research, customer identification, and competitive analysis.

Months 4–9

Execution Phase

Prototype development and securing the first customers.

Throughout

Key Principle

"Building cheap and learning fast," with momentum accountability checks.

The Result

A pivot to a B2B institutional model targeting clubs and federations, capitalizing on Olympic preparation cycles, with a working prototype already in live testing. The founders evolved into commercial leaders capable of disciplined decisions under uncertainty.
